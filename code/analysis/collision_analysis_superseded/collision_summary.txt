=== 7. Caveat ===
Collision behavior is conditional on the predefined sampling prior; the paired
construction of mu_n/mu_p, tau_n/tau_p, Nc/Nv is an intentional physically
constrained prior, not a defect. Directional claims are data-observational only.

=== 8. SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSIONS (data-only) ===

Q1. Do near-identical J-V curves exist with clearly different parameters?
    -> SUPPORTED. Of 7,666 deduplicated nearest-neighbor pairs in the test set,
       the closest have normalized-RMSE dJ ~ 4e-5 to 1e-3 (bottom 0.1-1%), far
       below any realistic measurement resolution, yet with median dTheta ~ 0.20
       (out of a [0,1]^9 log-param box). Pure single-parameter collisions exist:
       (i=1010,j=2641) differs only in tau_ETL (0.38 -> 0.99) at dJ pct 0.10%;
       (i=8271,j=10258) differs only in rad_c (0.71 -> 0.09) at dJ pct 0.99%.

Q2. How large is the ambiguity at near-identical J?
    -> SUPPORTED. The J->param constraint is weak: Spearman(dJ,dTheta)=0.158
       (p<1e-40, n=7666). Nearest-neighbor median dTheta (0.20) is only ~43%
       smaller than the random-pair baseline (0.35). Even at the closest 0.1%
       of pairs (n=8), median dTheta = 0.102 - i.e. ~0.1 in normalized log
       space despite effectively identical J-V.

Q3. Which parameters are most ambiguous in collision pairs?
    -> SUPPORTED (closest-1% median |diff|, normalized log space):
       tau_ETL (0.154) > mu_n (0.118) > tau_HTL (0.101) ~ tau_n (0.101) >
       rad_c (0.089) > tau_p (0.087) > mu_p (0.073) > Nv (0.060) > Nc (0.052).

Q4. Which parameters are most stable?
    -> SUPPORTED. Nc_eff (0.052) and Nv_eff (0.060) show the smallest median
       spread at closest-1% J-V, followed by mu_p (0.073).

Q5. Does standard J-V uniquely constrain all nine parameters?
    -> SUPPORTED (as a statement of non-uniqueness within this prior/domain).
       The pure interface and pure rad_c cases shift a single parameter by
       ~0.6 in normalized log space (~2-3 decades physically) while J-V stays
       in the bottom 0.1-1% of closest curves.

Q6. Is parameter recoverability heterogeneous?
    -> SUPPORTED. Recovery r spans 0.56-0.92 and collision-ambiguity spans
       0.05-0.15; both place tau_ETL worst and Nc/Nv best. The two rankings
       are consistent but not identical.

Q7. Is sensitivity equivalent to identifiability / recoverability?
    -> SUPPORTED (they are NOT equivalent). Three distinct measures disagree:
       tau_ETL has the lowest J-sensitivity (0.015) and the worst collision
       ambiguity (0.154) but only mid-tier recoverability (r=0.59); rad_c has
       mid sensitivity (0.033) and mid ambiguity (0.089) yet the LOWEST
       recoverability (r=0.56); mu_n has the HIGHEST sensitivity (0.259) yet
       high collision ambiguity (0.118). Sensitivity is necessary but not
       sufficient for recoverability, and neither equals collision-ambiguity.

=== 9. COLLISION MECHANISMS (observational, closest-1% pairs) ===
   - Bulk <-> interface recombination compensation: tau_n vs tau_ETL / tau_HTL
     move in OPPOSITE directions in ~75% of closest pairs (dominant mechanism).
   - Radiative <-> non-radiative compensation: rad_c vs tau_n opposite in 62%;
     rad_c vs tau_ETL opposite in 75%.
   - Paired (avg+ratio) parameters mostly move together as expected from the
     construction: tau_n/tau_p same-sign 88%, mu_n/mu_p 62%; Nc/Nv 50%.
   Caveat: collision behavior is conditional on the predefined sampling prior;
   the paired construction is an intentional physically constrained prior, not
   a defect. These are data-observational patterns, not causal proof.
