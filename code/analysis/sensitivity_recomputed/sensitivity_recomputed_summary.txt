ADOPTED VERSION:  S_log (log10 parameter space).
  Rationale: the model is trained and evaluated in normalized-log10 parameter space
  (log_transform_mask all True; pre-processing applies log10 before MinMax); the latent
  parameters span decades (mu_n 4.9e-7..2.2e-3, tau 4.5e-10..2e-2, N 4.5e22..2.2e26);
  a correlation in log space therefore measures sensitivity on the scale the network
  actually uses, whereas S_raw is dominated by the largest absolute parameter.
  Ranks largely agree between the two versions (see CSV); the log-space version is the
  scientifically meaningful one and is reported in the manuscript.
